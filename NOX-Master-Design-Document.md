# NOX Master Design Document

Author: Donald Heese

Status: Active Working Draft

---

# Purpose

NOX is an independent research program focused on hostile
agentic artificial intelligence containment, governance,
verification, assurance, and operational control.

---

# Core Principles

- Environmental Certainty Precedes Behavioral Measurement
- Understanding Must Be Verified Before Authority Is Granted
- Governance Is More Trustworthy Than Cognition
- Decision Provenance Is Required For Operational Trust
- Residual Opacity Must Be Measured And Managed

---

# Core Architectural Components

## Hostile Agentic Artificial Intelligence (HAI)

Working threat taxonomy.

## Environmental Understanding Verification (EUV)

Environmental certification framework.

## Cognitive Governance Kernel (CGK)

Supervisory governance architecture.

## Decision Provenance

Traceability of cognition-to-action pathways.

## Cognitive Resource Governance

Control of planning depth, memory, tools, and compute resources.
